PART A — Executive-summary alignment (framing only)
A.1 The defect this fixes
The document contains a leading analysis, §12.5.1.3.1, whose finding is that the effect of interest is the vertical gap between the fixed-study-set and study-tracking curves: at a 1 µm floor the river RQ is 0.633 with the study set fixed but 9.403 when it tracks the floor (a ~15× study-inclusion effect), because the food-dilution HC5 collapses from ~918 to ~68 particles/L when <10 µm-tested studies enter the SSD; coherent extrapolation alone leaves the river RQ below 1 1. Yet the executive prose still asserts, unqualified, that "Food Dilution is risk-dominant; Tissue Translocation is below threshold at the median, for both matrices" 1 and that "Food Dilution remains the risk-dominant ERM… consistent with the qualitative conclusion of the prior revision," with residual magnitude uncertainty framed around "which of the three convention fixes is applied" and "the decomposition in Appendix A." 1 The summary contradicts, or at least under-represents, the document's own new result, and it still foregrounds the slope-convention fixes as the principal magnitude uncertainty when the salient ones are now the lower size floor, the study-inclusion rule, and the sub-floor extrapolation model.
A.2 Target framing (numbers unchanged, glue-pulled)
Rewrite the top-line conclusion to the method-conditional / cannot-be-excluded register:
Food-dilution threshold exceedance cannot be excluded but is not robust. At the field-standard 1 µm floor, the food-dilution RQ exceeds 1 in both matrices (river MC2D median RQ = 7.88, P(RQ>1) = 100%; ocean = 3.28, P(RQ>1) = 73.8%) 1, while tissue translocation is below threshold (river 0.194; ocean 0.037) 1. This exceedance is conditional: it collapses below 1 as the lower size floor rises from 1 to 2.5 µm, and §12.5.1.3.1 shows it is generated by admitting <10 µm-tested toxicity studies into the volume-based food-dilution SSD (a hazard/HC5 effect) rather than by coherent sub-10 µm exposure extrapolation. With the study set held fixed and exposure and hazard rescaled coherently, the river food-dilution RQ at a 1 µm floor is 0.633 (1D) 1 and 0.986 under MC2D, with ocean below 1 under both metrics (1D = 0.627, MC2D = 0.326) 1. Because the ~68 particles/L food-dilution HC5 reproduces the published field-standard value, this fragility is a property of the standard method, not of these sites. Tissue translocation is robustly below threshold across all floors. The exceedance's magnitude is further bracketed by two exposure-model sensitivities — the two-segment (piecewise) fit (§13.2) and the MLE/lognormal small-end model (§12.5.2, Part B) — reported in the robustness section.
Mandatory caveats carried into the summary:
1.	Magnitude vs threshold-crossing — preserve verbatim in spirit: sub-10 µm exposure de-extrapolation is the larger single lever by magnitude (exposure-CF fold 79.86× vs composition 14.86×); read this as "study inclusion is decisive for threshold-crossing," never "extrapolation is irrelevant." 1
2.	TT caveat — where TT appears in the study-inclusion framing, keep: the TT M12equiv reference carries the ≥10 µm composition-freeze caveat: it is a lower bound on TT hazard, not a TT risk estimate 1.

The section therefore poses its question but does not answer it. Part B completes it.

B.3 Interpretation the section must land
Frame the three exposure-model results as a coherent bounding set with the piecewise result:
•	If the lognormal RQ stays > 1 → the exceedance is robust to the extrapolation model (strengthens the "cannot be excluded" statement).
•	If it falls below 1 → the exceedance is not robust to a defensible small-end roll-off; state plainly, alongside the 2.5 µm floor collapse and the piecewise river FD RQ ≈ 1.08 (§13.2 / SPEC 2b), as convergent evidence that the food-dilution exceedance sits on the unconstrained sub-floor zone.
•	Either way, connect to the mechanistic reading already in the document: food dilution is a large-particle (volume/gut-filling) mechanism while small particles preferentially drive translocation; admitting small-particle studies into the volume-based food-dilution SSD is the plausible reason the HC5 collapses — this tests the effect, not that specific mechanism, directly 1.
•	Note honestly that GoF does not favor the roll-off: the power law is not rejected for either matrix 1, so the lognormal is an admissible alternative, not a preferred one; the honest statement is the RQ range across admissible models, weighted by the (non-)discriminating Vuong result.
